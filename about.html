Meet Michael – Your Private Party Entertainer

Michael is an independent entertainer bringing professional, personalized private shows to Folly Beach and Charleston. Perfect for bachelorette parties, birthdays, or small private events, his performances focus on fun, excitement, and memorable experiences.
